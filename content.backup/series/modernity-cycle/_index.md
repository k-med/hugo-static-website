## The Modernity Cycle

This series explores the **moral disintegration of modern civilisation** and the possibilities for its renewal.  
It contrasts ancient notions of *virtue, reciprocity, and civic duty* with today’s hyper-monetised world — asking what must be recovered if the West is to regain dignity.

*Featured essays include:*

- **The Collapse of Non-Monetary Value Systems in the Modern West**  
  _When trust becomes currency and citizenship becomes transaction._

- **Upcoming:** _The Cult of Utility and the Death of Beauty_  
  _On how efficiency replaced excellence as the Western ideal._
