The beauty of Linux is sovereignty — but sovereignty without systems collapses into tinkering hell.  
Most users build their desktop environments by hand, layering tools until entropy wins. This project flips that.

Instead of chasing dotfile aesthetics, the goal is **automation with taste**: a clean Hyprland setup that respects minimalism, composability, and privacy.  
One script bootstraps your base Arch install (with full disk encryption and Btrfs). The second configures everything else after reboot — Hyprland, Waybar, Wofi, Pipewire, themes, fonts, dotfiles, and even sane defaults for `zsh` and `starship`.

It’s not meant to be “yet another ricer repo.”  
It’s a reproducible, repairable foundation — a reminder that the most elegant systems are the ones you can rebuild from scratch in an afternoon.

> _“Freedom begins where automation ends, but mastery begins where you automate what you already understand.”_
