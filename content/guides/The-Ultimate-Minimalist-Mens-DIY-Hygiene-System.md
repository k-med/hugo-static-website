# **Core Ingredients (Cheap, Natural, Effective)**

These do 99% of the work:

* **Baking soda (bicarb)** – deodorant, toothpaste, shampoo, scrub
* **Salt / Epsom salt** – disinfect, exfoliate, hair texture
* **Vinegar** – deodorizer, scalp rinse
* **Coconut or olive oil** – moisturiser, shave oil, beard oil
* **Plain bar soap** – body, hair, clothing
* **Cornstarch** *(optional)* – dry shampoo, anti-chafe
* **Alcohol** *(optional)* – tool sterilisation

# **DIY Hygiene Recipes (Fast & Effective)**

## **Deodorant**

**Daily:**

* Pinch of baking soda under each arm.

**Strong:**

* Baking soda + a little coconut/olive oil.

**Emergency:**

* Saltwater wipe.

---

## **Teeth & Mouth**

**Toothpaste:**

* Wet brush → dip in baking soda.

**Mouthwash:**

* Warm saltwater
* or diluted vinegar

---

## **Face & Skin**

* Wash with soap or saltwater
* Light oil to moisturise
* Bicarb paste or salt scrub to exfoliate

---

## **Hair**

**Shampoo:**

* 1 tsp baking soda in warm water → pour on scalp

**Conditioner:**

* 1–2 tbsp vinegar in a cup of water

**Texture / style:**

* Water + salt in spray bottle = instant sea-spray look

**Dry shampoo:**

* Cornstarch or bicarb

---

## **Beard**

* Wash with soap
* Condition with 1 drop oil
* Style with oil + tiny bit of wax (optional)

---

## **Shaving**

* Coconut/olive oil as shaving lubricant
* Cool water rinse + vinegar water dab
* Oil to prevent irritation afterward

---

## **Feet**

* Saltwater or vinegar wipe
* Baking soda in shoes overnight for odor

---

## **Clothes & Gear**

* Soap + water for washing
* Baking soda or vinegar for odor control
