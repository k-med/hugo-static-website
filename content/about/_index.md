This site is a public notebook — organized thoughts on building systems that matter and living examined lives under late-modern conditions.

## What You'll Find Here

**[Writing](/posts/)** organized into **[9 thematic series](/series/)**: from essays on civilizational decay to Linux privacy guides, from craft traditions to Stoic practice, from nomadic work strategies to civic renewal blueprints.

**[Projects](/projects/)**: Case studies of things actually built — physical, technical, or communal — with costs, outcomes, and lessons learned.

## Approach

- **Substance over scale**: Quality thought, not content churn
- **Practical philosophy**: Ideas tested against reality
- **Open tooling**: Hugo, PaperMod, Cloudflare Pages
- **Sovereignty by default**: No tracking, no ads, no newsletter popup hell
